paperus

One click on the paperus button saves the paper you are reading, with its PDF, to your library, or the PDF you have open. Right-click the button to save to another owner. On arXiv, a small button next to each paper does the same.

Not encrypted

This paperus address starts with http://: the extension's access token and the papers you save cross the network unencrypted, readable by others on it. Fine for a first try on a network you trust; before real use, ask for HTTPS (a certificate for the server) and connect to its https:// address.

Connect

paperus opens in a tab; confirm there. The extension then may add papers to your library and see which ones you have, nothing else.

Connected

To .

For another owner now and then: right-click the paperus button.

Papers you have already are recognised and not added twice.

Log

What the extension did, newest first: the page it read, each fetch and its answer, where a PDF link led. To find out why something failed; your paperus's administrators see it too (Administration → Logs). The last 20 steps are kept here, to copy into an issue.
